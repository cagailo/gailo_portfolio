About Me

Hello my name is Cyrill Ken Gailo or CK!, I am a Multimedia arts student at FEU TECH. I enjoy creating art in any medium that I find interesting. I am mainly a figure artist but i practice other mediums as well.

One of my interests is digital art and gaming. I enjoy creating characters, practicing animation, and learning how movement and design can bring ideas to life. I am also interested in photography because I like capturing interesting moments, places, and subjects through images. Currently I am trying to learn how to develop games bit by bit.

My goal is to continue improving my skills in photography, animation, design, and other areas of art. In the future, I would like to work in the creative filming industry, possibly as an indie film producer or anything related to it.
